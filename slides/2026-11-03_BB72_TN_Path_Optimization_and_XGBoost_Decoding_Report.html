Appendix B

Model selection and timing use explicit comparison boundaries

The source packages preserve predictions, hyperparameters and repeat-level timing.

Learning protocol

  • 12 binary classifiers. Joint 12-bit validation agreement selects the ensemble.
  • Per-bit early stopping monitors validation logloss.
  • V2 fixed recipe: 100 trials. Per-stage search: 40 trials per size.
  • V3: 40 multiobjective trials. Test and fresh natural shots reserved for final evaluation.

Execution protocol

  • CPU-only XGBoost training and inference. GPU TN produces teacher labels.
  • V4: 36 configurations, 180 repeats, one host / affinity session.
  • Each repeat times 1,000 single shots. Batch size 512 is a separate measurement.
  • Model loading, process startup and warmup are excluded. Prediction and IPC overhead are included.
Headline V4 P99 is the mean of five repeat P99 estimates, not a batch-amortized time.
S4-S6. Reused validation for tuning and early stopping is a model-selection limitation.15 / 16
